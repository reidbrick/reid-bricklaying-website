About us

More Than 25 Years of Experience. A Team You Can Rely On.

Reid Bricklaying is a family-owned and operated business with a proud history spanning more than 25 years.

Established by our father and now led by three brothers, we continue to build on the foundations of quality workmanship, reliability and strong relationships with our clients.

We specialise in high-end architectural residential brickwork and commercial masonry, working closely with builders, architects and project teams to deliver results that meet the design, programme and quality requirements of each project.

Our experienced workforce and hands-on approach allow us to take on projects of varying size and complexity. We focus on clear communication, efficient site coordination and careful execution, helping keep your project moving while maintaining a high standard of finish.

When you engage Reid Bricklaying, you work directly with an accountable team that understands the importance of getting the details right, coordinating effectively on site and delivering work that reflects the quality of the overall project.

Whether you are a builder seeking a dependable masonry contractor, an architect looking to realise a detailed design or a homeowner planning a distinctive residence, we bring the experience and care required to deliver quality brickwork with confidence.
